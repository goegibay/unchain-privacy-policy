# UNCHAIN Support

For help, bug reports, or questions about privacy and data handling, email:

**stevenhuang42195@gmail.com**

When possible, include:

- Your platform (iPhone or Android) and OS version;
- Your UNCHAIN version;
- The steps immediately before the issue occurred;
- A screenshot that contains no sensitive information.

We will never ask for your password, Apple Account password, or Google Account password.

## iOS Data Handling

The iOS edition requires no account and contains no ads, membership, subscription, or in-app purchase. Challenge, reflection, and Guardian data stay on the device. You can erase all local data in UNCHAIN Settings; deleting the app also removes data in its iOS sandbox.
